Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Contact Links+ helps you manage contacts, remove duplicates, create backups, save quick links, and add locations for your contacts. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information Used by the App

  • Contacts: The app requests access to your iPhone Contacts so you can view, edit, delete, merge, export, import, and back up contacts.
  • Saved locations: If you add a location to a contact, the latitude, longitude, and location name are stored on your device and may be included in full backups you create.
  • Quick links: Links such as WhatsApp, Instagram, websites, and other custom links are stored on your device and may be included in full backups you create.
  • Location permission: The app may use your current location to show nearby saved contact locations, calculate distance, or prefill a new saved location. Your current location is not stored unless you choose to save it as a contact location.
  • Clipboard: The app may check whether the clipboard contains a phone number or map link only when a relevant screen is opened. Clipboard content is used only when you choose to paste it.

Local Storage and Backups

Contact Links+ stores contact-related links and saved locations locally on your device. Full backup files are created only when you choose to export them. If you share, save, or move a backup file outside the app, you are responsible for keeping that file safe.

Analytics and Crash Reports

The app may use Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and improve reliability. Crash and diagnostic data may include device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and usage events. This data is used to improve the app and is not sold.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Contact data, saved locations, and quick links are not uploaded to our own servers. Some diagnostics and analytics data may be processed by Firebase/Google as described above.

Permissions

You can control Contacts and Location permissions from the iOS Settings app. Some features may not work if permissions are disabled.

Children's Privacy

Contact Links+ is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@contactlinks.in.
