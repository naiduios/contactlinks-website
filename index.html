01 — HOME

The people behind every plan.

Start with a useful snapshot of your world: contacts, places, links, Collections, quick actions, and nearby people—all together when you need them.

  • One clear connection overview
  • Quick actions for everyday tasks
  • Nearby places and people

02 — PEOPLE

More than a contact card.

Search your address book, filter it your way, and see the saved places and links connected to every person before you even open their details.

  • Fast contact search and filters
  • At-a-glance location and link counts
  • Built-in dialer

03 — CONTACT DETAILS

Everything about a person. Together.

Keep contact methods, quick links, saved places, and email addresses in one place. When it’s time to reach out, call, text, or open WhatsApp in one tap.

  • Call, message, and WhatsApp shortcuts
  • Social profiles and custom quick links
  • Multiple saved places per contact

04 — PLACES

See who’s nearby.

Keep meaningful locations with the people they belong to. Explore them on a map, find what is nearby, and call, message, or get directions without losing your place.

  • All saved places on one map
  • Nearby places sorted by distance
  • Call, WhatsApp, and directions shortcuts

05 — COLLECTIONS

Collections for what matters.

Gather the places, links, and details for a trip, event, project, favorite routine, or anything else you want to keep in focus.

  • Create focused views for plans and projects
  • Search Collections, places, or links
  • See what every Collection contains

06 — COLLECTION DETAILS

A place for every detail.

Open a Collection to see its links, locations, and notes together. Add what you need, edit as plans change, and share a location when it is time to go.

  • Links, locations, and notes in one view
  • Add and edit as plans evolve
  • Share locations directly from the Collection

07 — AUTOMATIC BACKUP

Every connection. Safely kept.

Protect your latest changes automatically. Keep an encrypted backup on this iPhone, or add an encrypted copy in a Files folder you choose—complete with Collections.

  • Automatic backup toggle
  • On-device or Files folder destination
  • Contacts, places, links, and Collections

08 — IMPORT

A better address book starts here.

Review a backup before it changes your phone. Choose which contacts, locations, quick links, and Collections to bring in, while skipping duplicate contact data.

  • Review your import first
  • Select each data type independently
  • Merge Collections with what is already yours

09 — EXPORT HISTORY

Your connections. Always yours.

Review previous full backups at a glance. Every export makes its contents clear, including your contacts, locations, links, and Collections.

  • Clear backup date and size
  • Counts for every kind of data
  • A portable copy when you need it

10 — YOUR WAY

Built around your life.

Make Contact Links feel at home on your iPhone. Choose a theme, select Apple Maps or Google Maps for shared locations, and manage your backups from one place.
