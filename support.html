Common Help Topics

  • Contacts are missing: Check that Contact Links has Contacts access in iPhone Settings.
  • Nearby places or distances are unavailable: Check Location access in iPhone Settings, then reopen the map.
  • Quick actions do not open: Confirm that the phone number, profile link, or custom URL is valid and that the required app is installed.
  • Duplicate cleanup: Review each suggested match and select only the contacts you want to merge.
  • Importing data: Review the import first, then choose contacts, saved locations, and quick actions separately. Matching data can be skipped.
  • Exporting data: Use VCF Export for contacts only. Use Full Backup to include contacts, saved locations, and quick actions.
  • Recovering or restoring: Use Recover Unlinked Data for saved locations or quick actions without a matching contact, or Restore Full Backup to import a complete Contact Links backup.
  • Changing the appearance: Choose Ocean, Forest, or Sunset from the Theme section in More.

Important Note

Contact changes may sync with iCloud or other contact accounts depending on your iPhone settings. Create a backup before deleting or merging contacts, and keep exported backup files somewhere you can access later.
