Contact Links+ Support

Need help with Contact Links+? We are here to help with contact backup, restore, duplicate cleanup, quick links, saved locations, and map features.

Contact Support

Email us at support@contactlinks.in.

When contacting support, please include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Common Help Topics

  • If contacts are not visible, check Contacts permission in iPhone Settings.
  • If distances or nearby locations are unavailable, check Location permission in iPhone Settings.
  • Use VCF export for contacts only.
  • Use Full Backup when you want to restore contacts, saved locations, and quick links.
  • Review duplicate contacts carefully before merging or deleting.

Important Note

Contact changes may sync with iCloud or other contact accounts depending on your iPhone settings. Please create a backup before deleting or merging contacts.
